01 / FOCUS

Reliable AI needs to know its own uncertainty.

My research interests lie at the intersection of embodied AI, vision-language models, and trustworthy machine learning. I'm especially interested in building intelligent systems that reason reliably and interact safely in complex physical environments — using conformal prediction and uncertainty estimates to help autonomous agents know when to act and when to ask for help, and grounding that reasoning in real hardware rather than simulation alone.

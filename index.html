About Me

I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, working in Frederike Duembgen's TRIO Lab. My research sits at the intersection of embodied AI, vision-language models, and trustworthy machine learning, with a particular interest in building intelligent systems that reason reliably and interact safely in complex physical environments. Prior to CMU, I received my B.E. in Robotics Engineering from Chongqing University in 2026.

Research Interests

  • Uncertainty Quantification & Safe AI: Leveraging conformal prediction and uncertainty estimates to build trustworthy autonomous agents that know when to act and when to ask for human assistance
  • Embodied AI & Robot Learning: Real-robot deployment of sampling-based trajectory optimization, camera calibration, and closed-loop control on manipulator arms
  • Vision-Language Models: Applying VLMs to navigation, perception, and multimodal reasoning tasks
